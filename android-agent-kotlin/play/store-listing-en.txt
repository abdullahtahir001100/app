Zenvora — Family & device safety companion

Keep a child's or family phone connected to your Zenvora parent dashboard.
View activity, notifications, location, and device health with clear consent
and on-device permissions — the same class of capabilities used by parental-control apps.

Features for parents / guardians:
• Pair with a secure dashboard token
• Stay connected in the background (battery-friendly)
• Optional device administrator for lock policies
• Notification and browsing visibility for family safety
• Call, SMS, and contacts visibility when you grant those permissions

Privacy: data goes only to your Zenvora workspace. Revoke permissions or uninstall anytime.
Support: see in-app / dashboard help. Privacy policy required on your public site before Play review.
